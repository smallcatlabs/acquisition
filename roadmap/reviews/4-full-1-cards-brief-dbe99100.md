     - The form gains a "Show as" control (cards or table), and `search_form.txt` is named among the goldens that move, in both Input and Done when.
     - Or `view.fields` is read apart from the fold, as `saved` and `saved_revision` are (`opened_from`, `mod.rs`). That departs from the design's §2 ("the fold gains `view.fields`") and should be said as a departure.
