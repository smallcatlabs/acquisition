`roadmap/briefs/tree-answers-design.md` at `62855b3f`: a builder could not start implementation as written; D4 awaits the owner’s ruling, and the route contract needs resolving.

- **§2, D2/D4 — changes what gets built.** The proposed routes cannot select every bucket’s exact members using the named terms while preserving its scope. `counts::place_terms` emits `id:<id>` and a league condition; `eval.rs` makes `id:` match a place’s parent too. Thus a tab holding one match directly and two in pages has a `place` bucket of one whose route returns three. Under `realm=all`, identical coordinates apart from realm also receive identical terms; existing tab routes distinguish them by narrowing the realm, which D2 explicitly forbids. **Smallest change:** specify an exact own-location selector and its route, covering parent/page separation and realm collisions; include any required exception to the standing realm or denominator rules in the owner’s ruling.

- **§3, done-when — changes what gets built.** The acceptance lines do not establish D3’s promised recount without a store read or from one evaluation. Today `Places::tabs` reads `Store::tabs_document`, and `Places::marked`, `Asked::matched` and `Asked::elsewhere` independently evaluate the query. Those paths could remain while the builder adds `place` counts, implements D4, records a timing and regenerates the references, satisfying the drafted checks without delivering D3. **Smallest change:** require a recount operation over the held document and shared tally, with evidence that it requires no store access and preserves the complete document’s output.

- **§2, D4/D5; §4 — changes what gets built.** Stable partitioning preserves each place’s order, but the existing pagination cannot directly supply an arbitrary window within several places. `Rows.after` and `Rows.limit` apply to the globally sorted union in `answer.rs`; with interleaved sort values, partitioning a limited answer can leave either place’s requested window incomplete. The spike’s `batch` avoids this by requesting every matching row of the selected places before slicing. **Smallest change:** explicitly retain that approach for this step and put per-place pagination out of scope, or define the additional windowing contract and its acceptance case.

- **§3, first done-when — wording.** Comparing every place’s `matched`, `own` and `listed` with “its own request’s answer” is not a defined comparison. In `places.rs`, `listed` is a Boolean affected by the place filter and listed descendants, `own` excludes pages, and folders have no request; `tests/tabs.rs` already demonstrates a tab with `matched=3` and `own=1`. **Smallest change:** state separate checks: `matched` against the place request, folders against child sums, `own` against the direct-location tally, and `listed` against the existing filter-and-descendant rule.

- **§1 and §3, measurements — wording.** The brief carries measurement outputs without the commands required by C174, and its proposed comparison lacks a reproducible invocation. The item count and query/listing timings are stated beside a record pointer; the future measurement leaves the tab, request, runner and timed interval unspecified. **Smallest change:** replace the historical outputs with commands or pointers to exact commands, and give the comparison’s inputs and invocation so the builder can name its tolerance before running it.

- **§2, D3; discovery commands — wording.** The only discovery command finds evaluation expressions but does not identify the full change surface. I ran it: it returns `places.rs:589`, `633`, `664` and `693`; the last evaluates a place selector rather than the searched query, and the command finds neither listing reads nor callers, document assertions or request schema descriptions. **Smallest change:** explain that distinction and add targeted searches for `Places::tabs`/`characters`, document consumers and tests, and request/help descriptions, including the MCP’s scope description in `params.rs`.
