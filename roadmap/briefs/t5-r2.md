## Goal

When this round is done, the daemon's lifecycle driver sleeps through
the daemon's `Clock`: its three timers (the idle deadline, the drain's
deadline, the quiet wait's look) and the exit's deadline are instants
on that clock, so on the parking clock they park and on the system
clock they run as today, to the same absolute deadlines; the exit
bound's OS thread stays real (C133). Every caller of the driver moves
with it in the same commit, since on the manual clock a driver on the
clock fires at once (the spike, `4-0-spike.md` "Answer"): the drain
scenarios whose inputs and outputs are the frontend boundary's run
through the door in virtual time; the rest, which stage the daemon's
internals or read them, stay component tests on a parking clock they
drive by hand, each saying so. The one elapsed lower bound on the
tokio drain becomes a bound on the clock. No production behaviour
changes but the clock a timer reads; no word a user meets changes; no
log line a test reads changes.
