# Decisions — Item search: the crate, the line, the query, the answer, the basis

Part of the decision registry (`AGENTS.md`, "Cross-cutting rulings", whose rules apply unchanged): the rulings that bind item search and the `acquisition-search` crate; the trade boundary's are `decisions/trade.md`. Read this before touching `search/` or the crate, with `search/README.md` (the slice's working directory, its rules and traps) and `search/DIGEST.md` (the `S` ids cited here, looked up by id and never read whole).

**Standing.** An entry in the owner's classes changes by his ruling, and a session writes and amends the rest, telling him what changed (C170, who decides). The language on one page is `acq search --describe`; how the lines were reached is `search/LEDGER.md`, "How the design was reached"; his words on each line and the dated trail are this file's history (`git log -p decisions/search.md`).

## The crate and the deriver (ruled)

- **C89 — Item search is its own library crate, `acquisition-search`, over the store's read API.** It links the store, and `acquisition-plan` once a query reads the effective price (C81, the effective price); never the daemon, the client, an HTTP client or an async runtime, and it opens no file of its own, so it is no third door (C12, two surfaces). The daemon and the planner never link it: a plan takes item ids, never a query. *Why:* each property is an edge the check can refuse, and a search edit recompiles no daemon. *Pinned:* `tools/arch-check.sh` (the edges).
- **C103 — One deriver per fact.** Place and every other ingest fact come from the store's read, joined as the store's own reads join them, never re-derived from a body; what a body says is derived in the search crate by a pure function of the body and those facts that names no store type, so it can move under the store unchanged. What the store gains for it is C108 (the corpus read). *Why:* two reads in one crate already meant different things by "in this league" (S131, two readings of place), and two derivers over one body is that bug across a crate line. *Pinned:* `acquisition-search/tests/derive.rs`.

## The line and the query

- **C97 — The vocabulary is a read of this corpus and the same call as search.** Counting by `line` under the query in hand lists the templates the matching items carry, by kind (and by realm under an all-realms scope), ranked by how many items carry each, with the range per slot; several texts may be asked in one call, and every row carries the term that selects it, ready to paste. The closed lists (fields, kinds, flags, totals with their definitions) are printed in the help. Autocomplete and an agent's schema discovery are this one read. *Why:* a human's autocomplete and an agent's discovery are one need. *Pinned:* `acquisition-search/tests/counts.rs`, `acquisition-search/tests/rows.rs`.

## The corpus and the answer

- **C98 — No search service: a consumer holds the corpus, and every answer is derived from and labelled with one consistent basis.** The basis names the store and account, one snapshot of facts with locations, the intent revision, the build, and the reference and derivation versions; a held corpus is reused only while every component it carries matches the basis of the answer in hand, checked before every answer. A write after the basis is chosen does not unmake a correctly labelled answer. A persisted projection is a candidate that waits for a measured failure. *Why:* a check is structure where an event is discipline (P5, structure over discipline), and a stale result taken for truth is C48's failure (no cached search). *Pinned:* `acquisition-search/tests/answer.rs` (the held corpus: `c98_the_holder_reuses_one_corpus_while_its_basis_and_scope_hold`).
- **C100 — There is one answer shape: the canonical query, basis, scope, terms block, total, a view and how to continue.** A row is compact by default (id, name, base, rarity, place by name and id, the lines the query touched with their values; C53, the decision view), and the caller may name fields; the body comes only through `show` (`--against`: the why-not). Every printed id is accepted back. A continuation names its basis (C98, one basis) and the query, view and order it continues, and is refused across a change. `priced` is the effective price (C81, the most specific statement), joined read-only. *Why:* an answer that carries its evidence needs no second call and no body. *Pinned:* `acquisition-search/tests/rows.rs`, `acquisition-search/tests/next.rs`.
- **C101 — A missing reach is a field to add, never a general door.** There is no raw-path accessor over the body and no general collection syntax. Socket colours, and abyssal sockets beside them, are asked over the whole item and within one link group. A pseudo that is not a sum or count over lines is a derived field, a named function over properties, lines and reference data (C68, reference tables), listed with its definition, never a total row (C94, named totals). Unmapped site filters stay an open catalogue, listed as gaps. *Why:* reach is a property of the derivation, and a derived field is the cheap class of change. *Pinned:* `acquisition-search/tests/percentile.rs`, `acquisition-search/tests/sockets.rs` (`c101_an_abyssal_socket_is_asked_by_its_word_over_the_item_and_in_a_group`), `acquisition-search/tests/trade_translate.rs` (its fixtures of the socket filters' `a`).
- **C102 — The digest's limits register is inherited with one exception, and each limit is an output.** What the search says on meeting a limit is the register's wording (`search/DIGEST.md`, "Limits register"); the exception is a moved template under a shipped total, which looks like a lacking one (C94, named totals). Binding occurrences does not say which twin is local; a site defence bound is inexact (C99, the translation); judgment of what is good, worth keeping or legacy is the user's (C106, no judgment). The search never fuzzy-matches, values, judges legacy, fetches, acts on the stash, or recovers the mod behind a line. *Why:* a limit stated at the answer is read; one in a document is not. *Pinned:* `acquisition-search/tests/refusal.rs`, `acquisition-search/tests/acceptance.rs`.
- **C106 — Reference data admits game data and community convention, never judgment.** Data is what the game files state (base ranges); convention is a concept with a published community definition (equipment, the trade site's categories). Both enter as reviewed tables (C68) from a registered surface (C79, governed surfaces), through the admission test the crate's doc states, and remove nothing: the user composes their own meaning from terms. Judgment (what is good, what it is worth, what to keep) is never embedded, and legacy stays the user's (C107, legacy is the user's). *Why:* a derived field needs data under it, and judgment embedded there would be the tool's, not the user's. *Pinned:* `acquisition-search/tests/class.rs`, `acquisition-search/tests/category.rs`.
- **C107 — Legacy is the user's word; a source's own label may be a field.** No field, term or output of the search says "legacy". What a registered source prints about an item, such as the variant label Path of Building gives a unique (`Pre 3.23.0`, `Current`), is convention under C106 (data and convention) and may back a derived field (C101, fields not doors) that prints the label and the source's version; an item that fits an old and a current variant, or none, is undecided with its reason (C93, absent versus undecided). *Why:* a source exists for a unique's variants, and none, for any item, says what the game can still produce. *Pinned:* `acquisition-search/tests/acceptance.rs`.
- **C117 — GGG's facts about a tab are fields, and the tool says nothing of what the site lists.** `tab.public` (the listing's `metadata.public`; a substash takes its tab's; an item on a character lacks it), `tab.remove_only` (the name suffix GGG adds, N47) and `price.side` are searchable. Whether the site would list an item is the agent's inference, labelled as such. *Why:* seat runs inferred "would a buyer see it" from `has:priced` and `price show` while the store held the flag. *Pinned:* `acquisition-search/tests/seat_faults.rs`, `acquisition-cli/tests/seat_faults.rs`.

## Not doing

What someone would propose and the project declines, each until the trigger it names or for good; the backlog is the repository's GitHub issues.

- A persisted projection or a totals batch: not until the empty-query load passes 500 ms release (`search/M3-LEDGER.md`); a fired trigger opens an experiment, never persistence by default.
- Whole-corpus export of the derived lines: not until a recorded question the language and the JSON answer cannot serve.
- A realm word inside a term: refused under C96; two searches are the workaround.
- Mod attribution (affix type, tier): not until a recorded question needs an affix count or a tier; C102 names it out of reach.
- Items that show a wording a release retired, counted or read as undecided: not until `live`, a second league's regeneration that leaves more than a hundred such items in a store; a fetch cures each, and every answer says its places' fetch age.
- A value named by the question it asks, where it is named by its text as spelled, and a slot named by its own alternative of an or, where it is named by its whole group's selector: not until `seat` or `user`, a query met at the tool that shows a doubled column or a column holding a number its comparison did not bind; no match, count or order differs.

## Retired

Rulings retired to the comment under their id at each file that holds them; the first file named carries the reason, and an id here is never reused (`tools/bothy.py`, the retired rule).

- **C90 — A line's identity is (realm, kind, template), and the displayed text is the only name a line needs.** *Held by:* `crates/acquisition-search/tests/derive.rs`, `crates/acquisition-search/tests/rows.rs`, `crates/acquisition-search/tests/counts.rs`.
- **C91 — A query is one typed tree and a value: immutable, serializable, handed between a human and an agent and read from a site search.** *Held by:* `crates/acquisition-search/tests/language.rs`, `crates/acquisition-search/tests/answer.rs`.
- **C92 — A comparison on a line binds one occurrence; the item's total is asked for by name.** *Held by:* `crates/acquisition-search/tests/answer.rs`, `crates/acquisition-search/tests/language.rs`, `crates/acquisition-search/tests/seat_faults.rs`.
- **C93 — Absent is false; undecided is one outcome with a named reason; the two are never one count.** *Held by:* `crates/acquisition-search/tests/unread.rs`, `crates/acquisition-search/tests/answer.rs`, `crates/acquisition-search/tests/pseudo.rs`.
- **C94 — A named total is a sum that answers, defined once as reviewed reference data (C68, reference tables).** *Held by:* `crates/acquisition-search/tests/pseudo.rs`, `crates/acquisition-search/tests/ranged.rs`, `crates/acquisition-search/src/totals.rs`.
- **C95 — The counts view has two shapes and one optional sum; there is no aggregation language.** *Held by:* `crates/acquisition-search/tests/counts.rs`, `crates/acquisition-search/tests/explain.rs`.
- **C96 — Place is terms, but the realm is the search's scope, never a term, and a query never spans realms by default.** *Held by:* `crates/acquisition-search/tests/answer.rs`, `crates/acquisition-search/tests/language.rs`, `crates/acquisition-search/tests/counts.rs`.
- **C104 — The text is carried on every surface; the tree is accepted and always returned.** *Held by:* `crates/acquisition-search/tests/language.rs`, `crates/acquisition-search/tests/answer.rs`.
- **C105 — A count accounts for every matching item.** *Held by:* `crates/acquisition-search/tests/counts.rs`.
- **C109 — A query nests at most 32 deep, as text or as a tree; deeper is refused as `too_deep`.** *Held by:* `crates/acquisition-search/tests/depth.rs`.
- **C118 — An answer says how old its subject and each row are, and what kind of place it could not see.** *Held by:* `crates/acquisition-search/tests/seat_faults.rs`, `crates/acquisition-search/tests/answer.rs`, `crates/acquisition-cli/tests/seat_faults.rs`.
- **C125 — A table drawn from the game's export names the export it was drawn from, read from the clone and never typed, and the gate refuses a clone that has moved past the pin.** *Held by:* `tools/game-diff.py`, `tools/sources.py`, `tools/trade-check.sh`.
