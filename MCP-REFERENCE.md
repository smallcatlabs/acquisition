# `acq-mcp` — tool reference

Generated by `crates/acquisition-mcp/tests/mcp_reference.rs` from the server's own `initialize` instructions and `tools/list`; a golden file the test keeps equal to the binary. Do not edit: change the tool's attributes in `crates/acquisition-mcp/src/main.rs` and regenerate with `ACQ_UPDATE_FIXTURES=1 cargo test -p acquisition-mcp --test main mcp_reference`. The CLI's verbs are `CLI-REFERENCE.md`.

## Instructions (sent at `initialize`)

Acquisition (provider: mock). - price_set, price_clear and set_sync_policy write locally and send nothing. - submit_job, apply_plan and cancel_job talk to the daemon; in real mode a human starts it and this server never spawns one. - list_jobs, job_status, job_result and daemon_status observe, and answer without one: the jobs from the ledger on disk. - Every other tool reads locally, no daemon; refresh_plan adds a running daemon's quote, never starting one. - Begin with search and no arguments; where the store holds several realms, name scope.realm (the refusal offers each). - describe with names explains the language and the answers: fields first, then answer, trade, pricing, daemon, store; the whole is over 150 KB. - A refusal is marked isError: error, kind, and readings, next and offers where it has them; any request an offer or an answer holds is sent back as printed, to the tool it names or the same one. - No tool says what an item is worth: a price is the owner's own listing.

## Tools

### `accounts`

Accounts this machine has logged into (from the store's non-secret index; no daemon), each with its store file: `total` counts every one, `accounts` lists ten, `expand` every one, and `left_out` counts the rest. Each age is in words beside its time (`describe` with names ["ages"]).

- `expand` (boolean | null) — List every account, not ten.

### `apply_plan`

Execute a reviewed plan, its actions and nothing else, as one `apply` parent job the daemon admits or refuses whole. Refused offline if the stored policy revision moved since the plan (replan with refresh_plan). A plan spends each request once: an action fetched since the plan, or held by the daemon, is listed in `spent` and not sent, so a replay answers `applied: false` with `remaining_requests`, what a plan compiled now would fetch. Returns `job_id`, `account`, `requests`, `spent` and `skipped`; poll job_status, then job_result. Ages in words: `describe` ["ages"].

- `account` (string | null) — A username, with or without its #discriminator, or a uuid; needed only when several are known.
- `max_requests` (integer | null) — Refuse at admission — before any child job exists — if the plan authorizes more logical requests than this.
- `plan` (object, required) — The plan envelope exactly as refresh_plan returned it (the `plan` field). Re-validated here; a tampered or hand-built envelope will not parse.

### `cancel_job`

Cancel a waiting or running job; cascades to its descendants.

- `id` (integer, required) — The job id, as returned by submit_job.

### `characters`

Characters known to the store (no daemon), counted first — how many, fetched, fetched empty, never fetched, their items — then listed: ten, `limit` more or fewer, `expand` every one, each with class, level, league, `item_count` (the live items held at it now) and `fetched_items` (what the last full fetch carried, null until one), and when it was listed and fetched, each age in words beside its time. `describe` with names ["store"] says what each count counts, and ["ages"] what an age's words say. Fresh data is a `characters` or `character` job.

- `account` (string | null) — A username, with or without its #discriminator, or a uuid; needed only when several are known.
- `expand` (boolean | null) — List every character of the selection.
- `league` (string | null) — Restrict to one league; omitted lists every league.
- `limit` (integer | null) — List this many characters in place of ten, in the store's order.
- `realm` (string | null) — Restrict to one realm (pc, xbox, sony, poe2); omitted lists every realm.

### `daemon_status`

The daemon running: its identity — contract, executable and hash, the world it serves, its socket — which of the four match this server (`contract_matches`, `artifact_matches`, `provider_matches`, `world_matches`; `wanted` is this server's own) and, when compatible, its vitals. Observes only: `running: false` with no daemon, `compatible: false` for one of another contract, artifact, provider or world (never replaced here), `responsive: false` for a wedged one; `describe` with names ["daemon"] says what each holds, and ["ages"] what an age's words say.

No arguments.

### `describe`

What the tools answer and the language `search` takes, read by name (no store read): answer, a search answer's keys; trade, what a site search becomes and its report; fields, what a query may ask; pricing, listings and the revision rule; daemon, what the job tools answer without one; store, what store_status and tabs count. As compact JSON (`acq --json` is a fifth larger) the whole is over 150 KB: computed is some 80 KB, fields and trade some 20 KB each, categories some 10 KB, answer and pricing some 5 KB each, the rest under 4 KB each. An unknown name is refused, listing every name it knows.

- `names` (array | null) — Sections, each by the key it answers under: fields, line, linked, values, composition, operators, slots, computed, totals, counts, explain, answer, categories, trade, not_built, limits, pricing, daemon, store — or a name within one (league, total_res, revision). Omitted: everything, over 150 KB.

### `item_events`

Item events (added/moved/changed/removed) from recent ingests (no daemon): the newest ten, `limit` more or fewer, beside how many were returned and whether the limit was reached; `summary: true` counts them by location instead, ten locations, `expand` every one. `describe` with names ["store"] says what each count counts, and ["ages"] what an age's words say.

- `account` (string | null) — A username, with or without its #discriminator, or a uuid; needed only when several are known.
- `expand` (boolean | null) — With `summary`, every location.
- `hours` (number | null) — How far back to look (default 24 hours).
- `limit` (integer | null) — The most events read, the newest first (default ten).
- `summary` (boolean | null) — The events counted by location in place of listed: ten locations.

### `job_result`

A finished job's outcome — payload or error — with its job_id and account, from the daemon or, with none running, the ledger on disk; an unfinished one is refused. A parent's failure carries `children` and `failed_children`. Payloads can be large — prefer the store tools for reading data.

- `id` (integer, required) — The job id, as returned by submit_job.

### `job_status`

One job's state, ETA and retries. A waiting job with a large ETA is the rate limiter holding, not a hang (holds can reach about 5 minutes). Observes only: a finished job, or any job with no daemon running, is answered from the ledger on disk (`describe` with names ["daemon"]; ["ages"] for an age's words).

- `id` (integer, required) — The job id, as returned by submit_job.

### `list_jobs`

Jobs the daemon lists, with states and ETAs, counted first over every job (`total`, `by_state`), then listed: ten open and the ten most recent finished, `expand` every one. Observes only, never spawning a daemon: with none running it answers from the ledger (`running: false`, `persisted`); `describe` with names ["daemon"] says what each holds and counts, and ["ages"] what an age's words say.

- `expand` (boolean | null) — List every job, open and finished, not ten of each.

### `price_clear`

Remove one target's own price row; what it covered falls back to the next row up, or to the game's price. Local only: it sends nothing. `if_revision` names the revision of the row it removes (price_show reads it). Returns the receipt — `target`, `written` null, `prior`, the row removed, `revision`, the one the clear left, and `undo`, the price_set that puts it back, a create naming no revision, sent as printed (or `no_undo`, why none).

- `account` (string | null) — A username, with or without its #discriminator, or a uuid; needed only when several are known.
- `if_revision` (integer | null) — The revision of the target's own row this clear removes, as for price_set. Required: a clear without it is refused naming the current revision.
- `target` (string, required) — The address, as for price_set.

### `price_list`

The items of one league that are priced, each with both sides of its listing (no daemon): `items`, ten by default (`limit` more, `expand` all), each with its `subject`, the hand row that applies (`manual`), the game's reading (`game`), the `relation` and the `effective` price; `containers`, the tabs, substashes and characters they sit under; and the counts `describe` with names ["pricing"] explains. With neither `relation` nor `effective`, items nothing prices are left out, except unresolved ones.

- `account` (string | null) — A username, with or without its #discriminator, or a uuid; needed only when several are known.
- `covered_by` (string | null) — Only items a row on this target would cover: a folder's tabs' items, a map or unique tab's substashes' items too.
- `effective` (string | null) — Only items whose price this side decides: game, manual, none or unresolved.
- `expand` (boolean | null) — List every item of the selection.
- `in` (string | null) — Only items physically in this container: a tab, substash or character address.
- `league` (string | null) — The league; omitted, the league of the container `in` or `covered_by` names, else Standard.
- `limit` (integer | null) — List this many items in place of ten, the first in listing order; `total` says how many the selection holds.
- `realm` (string | null) — pc (the default), xbox, sony or poe2; a tab or substash address given to `in` or `covered_by` carries its own.
- `relation` (string | null) — Only items in this relation: none, manual_only, game_only, agree or conflict.

### `price_set`

Set one target's price by hand: one row on an item, tab, substash or character, which what it covers inherits unless a row nearer the item says otherwise. Local only: it sends nothing and changes nothing in game. Over a row the target already has, `if_revision` names that row's revision (price_show reads it); with none, omit it to create one. Returns the receipt — `target`, `written`, `prior`, `revision` (the next write's), `undo` (a request that puts `prior` back) or `no_undo` (why none), `in_facts`; `describe` with names ["pricing"] gives the revision rule and the undo.

- `account` (string | null) — A username, with or without its #discriminator, or a uuid; needed only when several are known.
- `amount` (string | null) — For exact and negotiable: a decimal of up to four places (`12.5`), or a `wanted/lot` ratio (`1/5`), as a string.
- `currency` (string | null) — For exact and negotiable: a tag of reference_currency.
- `if_revision` (integer | null) — The revision of the target's own row this write replaces: price_show's `listing.manual.revision`, when `listing.manual.from` is the target itself, or the revision a refusal names. Required when the target has a row of its own; omit it, or give 0, only to create one.
- `target` (string, required) — One address: `item/<id>`, `character/<id>`, `tab/<realm>/<id>` or `substash/<realm>/<parent>/<id>`. An answer holds a target as an object, `{scope, realm, parent, id}`: the address is its parts in that order joined by `/`, the absent ones left out.
- `type` (string, required) — exact, negotiable, no_price or skip (the game's `price`, `b/o` and `~skip` work too).

### `price_show`

One target's listing (no daemon): `listing`, both sides with their causes — the hand row that applies (`manual`, its `revision` and the target it is on, `from`), the note and the tab name verbatim beside their readings — the relation, the effective price and why; `container`, the one an item sits in; for a container, `items_here` (in it) and `items_covered_below` (through its children), ten each beside its counts, `limit` more, `expand` all (`describe` with names ["pricing"]; ages, ["ages"]). Read it before price_set or price_clear on a target with its own row, whose revision they name.

- `account` (string | null) — A username, with or without its #discriminator, or a uuid; needed only when several are known.
- `expand` (boolean | null) — List every item of both sets.
- `league` (string | null) — The league to read the target in; omitted, the league the facts hold it in, so an address an answer printed reads back as printed.
- `limit` (integer | null) — List this many of each item set in place of ten, the first in listing order.
- `realm` (string | null) — The realm of an item or character target (pc when omitted); a tab or substash address carries its own, and a realm given beside it must agree.
- `target` (string, required) — One address: `item/<id>`, `character/<id>`, `tab/<realm>/<id>` or `substash/<realm>/<parent>/<id>`. An answer holds a target as an object, `{scope, realm, parent, id}`: the address is its parts in that order joined by `/`, the absent ones left out.

### `price_status`

The listing state of one league, counted (no daemon): what every item, tab and character is priced as — by hand, in game, how the two stand — as the counts and the row accounting, without the listings themselves; `describe` with names ["pricing"] says what each count counts. price_list names the items; price_show is one target. Each age is in words beside its time (`describe` with names ["ages"]).

- `account` (string | null) — A username, with or without its #discriminator, or a uuid; needed only when several are known.
- `expand` (boolean | null) — List every row of `rows.unmatched`, `rows.unreadable` and `rows.other_league`, past the first ten.
- `league` (string | null) — The league; Standard when omitted.
- `realm` (string | null) — pc (the default), xbox, sony or poe2.

### `reference_currency`

The currency table this build ships (no store, no daemon): every row — `tag`, the word a price cites; `emit`, the word the game writes in a note; `aliases`, legacy words that resolve to it; `display`; `retired` where the game no longer writes it; `evidence` — with the table's version. `word` resolves one word instead.

- `word` (string | null) — A tag, the game's word, or a legacy alias to resolve, exact and case-sensitive; omitted, the whole table.

### `refresh_plan`

Compile a refresh plan for one realm and league, offline — sends nothing, spends nothing: from the stored sync policy, or with `selection` from that explicit selection, which needs no policy. The plan is the bounded action set applying would execute, with reasons, skips, unknown ids and a wire estimate; a running daemon adds its read-only quote, never spawned for it (`quote_note` says why one is absent). A policy plan's `notes` names a league the fetched league list lacks, or a covered league with no character listed. Review it, then hand `plan` to apply_plan. Its ages: `describe` ["ages"].

- `account` (string | null) — A username, with or without its #discriminator, or a uuid; needed only when several are known.
- `league` (string | null) — League name; defaults to "Standard".
- `realm` (string | null) — Realm: pc (default), xbox, sony, or poe2 (a poe2 plan can carry character work only).
- `selection` (object) — An explicit selection, compiled in place of the stored policy: {"tabs": "all" or [tab ids], "characters": [character ids], "deep": true to fetch a named map or unique tab's pages on record with it}, any of them left out; ids as `tabs` and `characters` list them.

### `search`

Item search over the store (no daemon): a query in the language `describe` prints, asked of every item in scope. The arguments are the request: {} is every item; {"query": {"text": "class:ring rarity=rare"}} is a query, and {"query": {"trade": a trade site URL}} a site search translated to one. The answer is one JSON document — `query`, `scope`, `total`, `rows`, `view`, `terms`, `basis`; `describe` with names ["answer"] says what each key holds and what each count counts. Any request an answer holds is sent back as printed.

- `basis` (object) — Carried by a continuation, as an answer's `next` holds it; never written by hand.
- `context` (string) — "matches" (the default), or "corpus": the view over every item in scope, the query set aside.
- `count` (integer) — Beside `request`, the count it was printed with: the answer then says under `before` where the count has moved.
- `counted_at` (object) — Beside `request` and `count`, the `basis` of the answer the count came from: the answer then says under `before` wherever the basis has moved.
- `query` (object) — {"text": a query in the language `describe` prints} or {"tree": the tree an answer returned}, never both; omitted, every item in scope. Or {"trade": a trade site search's URL, id or query JSON}, translated with no network; `describe` with names ["trade"] says what it becomes and reports.
- `request` (object) — In place of the fields above: a request as an answer holds one — a count's, `view.rows.next`, an offer — sent whole, as printed.
- `scope` (object) — What is searched: {"account": needed only where several could be meant, "realm": pc, xbox, sony, poe2 or all, needed only where the store holds several, "membership": "live" (the default), "all", the removed items too, each marked with when it left, or "removed"}.
- `view` (object) — {"rows": {"limit": N, "sort": a value, "desc": true}}, the default (ten rows); {"counts": {"keys": [a key, …], "sum": a value}}; {"cross": {"keys": [two keys]}}; or {"explain": {"path": a node's path}}; "routes": true beside any. `describe` with names ["answer"] says what each gives.

### `set_sync_policy`

Write the account's sync policy, validated strictly, compare-and-swap on `if_revision`. Local only: it sends nothing. Each realm the value names replaces that realm alone, the others stay; `replace: true` writes the whole policy. Returns the stored row with the receipt — `target`, `written`, `prior`, `undo` (a request that puts `prior` back) or `no_undo` (why none) — and `league_notes` for a league the fetched league list lacks, with `list_fetched_at` (null if never fetched; a `leagues` job fetches it) and its age in words (`describe` ["ages"]). Nothing refuses on a note.

- `account` (string | null) — A username, with or without its #discriminator, or a uuid; needed only when several are known.
- `if_revision` (integer | null) — The revision this write replaces, from sync_policy: required when a policy exists, `replace` or not; omitted, or 0, only to create the first.
- `replace` (boolean | null) — true writes the whole policy: every realm `value` does not name is removed. Default false: the realms `value` names, the rest kept.
- `value` (object, required) — The policy, e.g. {"version":3,"realms":{"pc":{"leagues":{"Standard":{"tabs":"all","characters":"all","max_age_seconds":3600}}}}}: per league `tabs` and/or `characters`, "all" or an id list; a realm named with no leagues is removed. Validated strictly, landing whole or not at all.

### `shop_render`

The forum shop pages for one league, for a person to paste by hand; this server posts nothing and reads no forum (no daemon). One link code per hand-priced item under its price's spoiler, in pages of at most `size` characters: `pages` (each `text` is what to paste), `posted`, `left_out`, `counts`, `policy` and `freshness`, whether the sync policy covers the posted items — reported, never enforced. `describe` with names ["pricing"] says what each count counts, and ["ages"] what an age's words say.

- `account` (string | null) — A username, with or without its #discriminator, or a uuid; needed only when several are known.
- `expand` (boolean | null) — List every entry of `posted`, `left_out` and `freshness`'s lists, past the first ten, the items nothing applies to included in `left_out` (a league holds thousands); they are counted either way.
- `league` (string | null) — The league; Standard when omitted.
- `page` (integer | null) — One page alone, by its number from 1: that page's record in place of the whole render.
- `realm` (string | null) — pc (the default), xbox, sony or poe2.
- `size` (integer | null) — Characters per page, the template included: the forum's hard limit when omitted.
- `template` (string | null) — The page's text, holding `[items]` exactly once, which each page replaces with its items; omitted, a page is the items alone.

### `show`

One item as the search derives it, live or removed (no daemon): its place, its fields, class, category and price, and every line with its kind, template and numbers. `against` adds why the item does or does not match a query, each node's outcome (`describe` with names ["against"]); `body` adds the stored body, verbatim. Each age is in words beside its time (`describe` with names ["ages"]).

- `account` (string | null) — A username, with or without its #discriminator, or a uuid; needed only when several are known.
- `against` (object) — Why the item does or does not match a query: `{"text": …}`, `{"tree": …}` or `{"trade": …}`, as a request's `query`.
- `body` (boolean | null) — Also return the body as the store holds it, verbatim.
- `id` (string, required) — An item's id, whole, as an answer printed it.

### `store_status`

Store file path, size, and row counts (no daemon); `describe` with names ["store"] says what each count counts.

- `account` (string | null) — A username, with or without its #discriminator, or a uuid; needed only when several are known.

### `submit_job`

Submit a job to the daemon and return its id at once; poll job_status, then job_result. `describe` with names ["daemon"] says what the job tools answer.

- `account` (string | null) — A username, with or without its #discriminator, or a uuid; needed only when several are known.
- `kind` (string, required) — Job kind, one a client may submit: profile, characters, character, leagues, stashes, stash, apply, sleep, fetch, whoami. sleep, fetch, whoami run against the mock daemon alone and are refused in real mode. apply_plan sends a reviewed plan, with the checks an apply here skips.
- `params` (object) — Kind-specific params, e.g. {"league": "Standard", "id": "..."} for stash. Passed verbatim; visible to every connected client.
- `priority` (integer | null) — Higher runs sooner (default 0).

### `sync_policy`

The account's sync policy (declared coverage and freshness) with its revision — what refresh_plan compiles (no daemon). `policy` is null when none is set. Each age is in words beside its time (`describe` with names ["ages"]).

- `account` (string | null) — A username, with or without its #discriminator, or a uuid; needed only when several are known.

### `tabs`

The stash tabs of a league, counted first, from the store (no daemon), then listed — the ten with the most items, `limit` more or fewer, `expand` every one — each with its type, item count and fetch time, its age in words beside it; `type` and `never_fetched` select the tabs listed. `describe` with names ["store"] says what each count counts, and ["ages"] what an age's words say. A name ending ` (Remove-only)` is GGG's mark on a tab whose items can only be taken out.

- `account` (string | null) — A username, with or without its #discriminator, or a uuid; needed only when several are known.
- `expand` (boolean | null) — List every tab of the league, or of the selection.
- `league` (string | null) — League name; defaults to "Standard".
- `limit` (integer | null) — List this many tabs in place of ten: the most items first where some are left out, else in the listing's order.
- `never_fetched` (boolean | null) — Only tabs never fetched.
- `realm` (string | null) — Realm: pc (default), xbox, or sony.
- `type` (string | null) — Only tabs of this type, as GGG names it (UniqueStash, MapStash, PremiumStash …; `by_type` lists them).
