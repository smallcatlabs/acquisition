# `acq-mcp` — tool reference

Generated by `crates/acquisition-mcp/tests/mcp_reference.rs` from the server's own `initialize` instructions and `tools/list`; a golden file the test keeps equal to the binary. Do not edit: change the tool's attributes in `crates/acquisition-mcp/src/main.rs` and regenerate with `ACQ_UPDATE_FIXTURES=1 cargo test -p acquisition-mcp --test main mcp_reference`. The CLI's verbs are `CLI-REFERENCE.md`.

## Instructions (sent at `initialize`)

Acquisition (provider: mock). - The store tools read the store, no daemon: search, show, describe, accounts, tabs, characters, store_status, item_events, sync_policy, refresh_plan. - The pricing tools and set_sync_policy write locally and send nothing. - The job tools talk to the daemon a human started (submit_job, apply_plan, list_jobs, job_status, job_result, cancel_job, daemon_status); in real mode this server never spawns one. - Begin with search and no arguments; where the store holds several realms, name scope.realm (the refusal offers each). - describe with names explains the language and the answers: fields first, then answer, trade, pricing, daemon; the whole is over 100 KB. - A refusal is a result marked isError, with readings and offers; any request an answer holds is sent back as printed. - No tool says what an item is worth: a price is the owner's own listing.

## Tools

### `accounts`

Accounts this machine has logged into (from the store's non-secret index; no daemon), each with its store file.

No arguments.

### `apply_plan`

Execute a reviewed plan, its actions and nothing else, as one `apply` parent job the daemon admits or refuses whole. Refused offline if the stored policy revision moved since the plan (replan with refresh_plan). A plan spends each request once: an action fetched since the plan, or held by the daemon, is listed in `spent` and not sent, so a replay answers `applied: false` with `remaining_requests`, what a plan compiled now would fetch. Returns `job_id`, `account`, `requests`, `spent` and `skipped`; poll job_status, then job_result.

- `account` (string | null) — A username, with or without its #discriminator, or a uuid; needed only when several are known.
- `max_requests` (integer | null) — Refuse at admission — before any child job exists — if the plan authorizes more logical requests than this.
- `plan` (object, required) — The plan envelope exactly as refresh_plan returned it (the `plan` field). Re-validated here; a tampered or hand-built envelope will not parse.

### `cancel_job`

Cancel a waiting or running job; cascades to its descendants.

- `id` (integer, required) — The job id, as returned by submit_job.

### `characters`

Characters known to the store, with class, level, league, and two counts — `item_count`, the live items the store holds at the character now, and `fetched_items`, what the last full fetch's body carried (equipment + inventory, socketed gems not counted), null until one — (no daemon). Fresh data is a `characters` or `character` job.

- `account` (string | null) — A username, with or without its #discriminator, or a uuid; needed only when several are known.
- `league` (string | null) — Restrict to one league; omitted lists every league.
- `realm` (string | null) — Restrict to one realm (pc, xbox, sony, poe2); omitted lists every realm.

### `daemon_status`

The daemon running: its identity — contract, executable and hash, the world it serves, its socket — which of the four match this server (`contract_matches`, `artifact_matches`, `provider_matches`, `world_matches`; `wanted` is this server's own) and, when compatible, its vitals. Observes only: `running: false` with no daemon, `compatible: false` for one of another contract, artifact, provider or world (never replaced here), `responsive: false` for a wedged one; `describe` with names ["daemon"] says what each holds.

No arguments.

### `describe`

What the tools answer and the language `search` takes, read by name (no store read): answer, every key of a search answer; trade, what a site search becomes and its report; fields, what a query may ask; pricing, the listing model and its revision rule; daemon, what the job tools answer without one. The whole is over 100 KB: computed is some 80 KB, fields and trade some 20 KB each, categories some 10 KB, answer some 5 KB, and the rest each under 4 KB. A name it does not know is refused, and the refusal lists every name it knows.

- `names` (array | null) — Sections, each by the key it answers under: fields, line, linked, values, composition, operators, slots, computed, totals, counts, explain, answer, categories, trade, not_built, limits, pricing, daemon — or a name within one (league, total_res). Omitted: everything, over 100 KB.

### `item_events`

Item events (added/moved/changed/removed) from recent ingests, newest first (no daemon).

- `account` (string | null) — A username, with or without its #discriminator, or a uuid; needed only when several are known.
- `hours` (number | null) — How far back to look (default 24 hours).
- `limit` (integer | null) — Maximum rows (default 200).

### `job_result`

A finished job's outcome — payload or error — with its job_id and account, from the daemon or, with none running, the ledger on disk; an unfinished one is refused. A parent's failure carries `children` and `failed_children`. Payloads can be large — prefer the store tools for reading data.

- `id` (integer, required) — The job id, as returned by submit_job.

### `job_status`

One job's state, ETA and retries. A waiting job with a large ETA is the rate limiter holding, not a hang (holds can reach about 5 minutes). Observes only: a finished job, or any job with no daemon running, is answered from the ledger on disk (`describe` with names ["daemon"]).

- `id` (integer, required) — The job id, as returned by submit_job.

### `list_jobs`

Jobs the daemon lists, with states and ETAs: the open ones and the finished ones the ledger on disk holds. Observes only, never spawning a daemon: with none running it answers from the ledger (`running: false`, `persisted`); `describe` with names ["daemon"] says what each holds.

No arguments.

### `price_clear`

Remove one target's own price row; what it covered falls back to the next row up, or to the game's price. Local only: it sends nothing. `if_revision` names the revision of the row it removes (price_show reads it). Returns `target`, `written` null and `prior`, the row removed; price_set with the prior's value puts it back.

- `account` (string | null) — A username, with or without its #discriminator, or a uuid; needed only when several are known.
- `if_revision` (integer | null) — The revision of the target's own row this clear removes, as for price_set. Required: a clear without it is refused naming the current revision.
- `target` (string, required) — The address, as for price_set.

### `price_list`

The items of one league that are priced, each with both sides of its listing (no daemon): `items`, each with its `subject`, the hand row that applies (`manual`), the game's reading (`game`), the `relation` and the `effective` price; `containers`, the tabs, substashes and characters they sit under; `items_on_record`, how many items the league holds, so an empty answer says which nothing; `total`, how many the selection holds, and `listed`, how many `items` lists — fewer only under `limit`, the first in listing order, `containers` then being theirs alone. With neither `relation` nor `effective`, items nothing prices are left out, except unresolved ones.

- `account` (string | null) — A username, with or without its #discriminator, or a uuid; needed only when several are known.
- `covered_by` (string | null) — Only items a row on this target would cover: a folder's tabs' items, a map or unique tab's substashes' items too.
- `effective` (string | null) — Only items whose price this side decides: game, manual, none or unresolved.
- `in` (string | null) — Only items physically in this container: a tab, substash or character address.
- `league` (string | null) — The league; omitted, the league of the container `in` or `covered_by` names, else Standard.
- `limit` (integer | null) — List at most this many items, the first in listing order (some 1.3 KB an item); `total` says how many the selection holds.
- `realm` (string | null) — pc (the default), xbox, sony or poe2; a tab or substash address given to `in` or `covered_by` carries its own.
- `relation` (string | null) — Only items in this relation: none, manual_only, game_only, agree or conflict.

### `price_set`

Set one target's price by hand: one row on an item, tab, substash or character, which what it covers inherits unless a row nearer the item says otherwise. Local only: it sends nothing and changes nothing in game. Over a row the target already has, `if_revision` names that row's revision (price_show reads it); with none, omit it to create one. Returns the receipt — `target`, `written`, `prior`, `in_facts`; `describe` with names ["pricing"] gives the revision rule and the undo.

- `account` (string | null) — A username, with or without its #discriminator, or a uuid; needed only when several are known.
- `amount` (string | null) — For exact and negotiable: a decimal of up to four places (`12.5`), or a `wanted/lot` ratio (`1/5`), as a string.
- `currency` (string | null) — For exact and negotiable: a tag of reference_currency.
- `if_revision` (integer | null) — The revision of the target's own row this write replaces: price_show's `listing.manual.revision`, when `listing.manual.from` is the target itself, or the revision a refusal names. Required when the target has a row of its own; omit it only to create one.
- `target` (string, required) — One address: `item/<id>`, `character/<id>`, `tab/<realm>/<id>` or `substash/<realm>/<parent>/<id>`. An answer holds a target as an object, `{scope, realm, parent, id}`: the address is its parts in that order joined by `/`, the absent ones left out.
- `type` (string, required) — exact, negotiable, no_price or skip (the game's `price`, `b/o` and `~skip` work too).

### `price_show`

One target's listing (no daemon): `listing`, both sides with their causes — the hand row that applies (`manual`, with its `revision` and the target it is on, `from`), the note and the tab name verbatim beside what each reads as — the relation, the effective price and why; `container`, the one an item sits in; for a tab, substash or character, `items_here` (physically in it) and `items_covered_below` (covered through its children). Read it before price_set or price_clear on a target with a row of its own: they name the revision they replace.

- `account` (string | null) — A username, with or without its #discriminator, or a uuid; needed only when several are known.
- `league` (string | null) — The league to read the target in; omitted, the league the facts hold it in, so an address an answer printed reads back as printed.
- `realm` (string | null) — The realm of an item or character target (pc when omitted); a tab or substash address carries its own, and a realm given beside it must agree.
- `target` (string, required) — One address: `item/<id>`, `character/<id>`, `tab/<realm>/<id>` or `substash/<realm>/<parent>/<id>`. An answer holds a target as an object, `{scope, realm, parent, id}`: the address is its parts in that order joined by `/`, the absent ones left out.

### `price_status`

The listing state of one league, counted (no daemon): what every item, tab and character is priced as — by hand (a row this server or the CLI wrote), in game (an item's note, then its tab's name, read only where the tab is public), how the two stand — as the counts and the row accounting, without the listings themselves. `counts.by_effective` says who decides each item's price (game, manual, none, unresolved — a row that cannot be read could decide), `counts.by_relation` how the two sides stand (none, manual_only, game_only, agree, conflict), so the items priced by hand are its manual_only, agree and conflict together; `counts.game_priced` the items the game prices; `counts.residue` the items carrying price text the trade site cannot see — a note on a character's item, which decides no price until the item is moved into a public stash tab, or a note or tab name in a tab that is not public, which decides none until the tab is made public in the game; `counts.invalid_notes` the notes that start like a price and do not read as one (no effect: the tab's name applies); `rows` accounts for every hand-written row: how many apply here, which name nothing in the facts (`unmatched`, each row whole — `value`, `from`, `revision`, `updated_at`, `written_via` — since no listing carries it), which cannot be read, which belong to other leagues or realms. price_list names the items; price_show is one target.

- `account` (string | null) — A username, with or without its #discriminator, or a uuid; needed only when several are known.
- `league` (string | null) — The league; Standard when omitted.
- `realm` (string | null) — pc (the default), xbox, sony or poe2.

### `reference_currency`

The currency table this build ships (no store, no daemon): every row — `tag`, the word a price cites; `emit`, the word the game writes in a note; `aliases`, legacy words that resolve to it; `display`; `retired` where the game no longer writes it; `evidence` — with the table's version. `word` resolves one word instead.

- `word` (string | null) — A tag, the game's word, or a legacy alias to resolve, exact and case-sensitive; omitted, the whole table.

### `refresh_plan`

Compile a refresh plan for one realm and league, offline — sends nothing, spends nothing: from the stored sync policy, or with `selection` from that explicit selection, which needs no policy. The plan is the bounded action set applying would execute, with reasons, skips, unknown ids and a wire estimate; a running daemon adds its read-only quote, never spawned for it (`quote_note` says why one is absent). A policy plan's `notes` names a league the fetched league list lacks, or a covered league with no character listed. Review it, then hand `plan` to apply_plan.

- `account` (string | null) — A username, with or without its #discriminator, or a uuid; needed only when several are known.
- `league` (string | null) — League name; defaults to "Standard".
- `realm` (string | null) — Realm: pc (default), xbox, sony, or poe2 (a poe2 plan can carry character work only).
- `selection` (object) — An explicit selection, compiled in place of the stored policy: {"tabs": "all" or [tab ids], "characters": [character ids], "deep": true to fetch a named map or unique tab's pages on record with it}, any of them left out; ids as `tabs` and `characters` list them.

### `search`

Item search over the store (no daemon): a query in the language `describe` prints, asked of every item in scope. The arguments are the request: {} is every item; {"query": {"text": "class:ring rarity=rare"}} is a query, and {"query": {"trade": a trade site URL}} a site search translated to one. The answer is one JSON document — `query`, `scope`, `total`, `rows`, `view`, `terms`, `basis` — and `describe` with names ["answer"] says what each key holds. Its counts: `scope` (account, realm, `items` every live item in scope before any term, what was and was not fetched: `fetched` counts the tabs, their pages and the characters fetched, `oldest_fetch` and `newest_fetch` the earliest and the latest of their last fetches, each one location's, where a row's `place.fetched_at` is its own; `never_fetched` counts the tabs, their pages and the characters a listing names and no fetch has filled, and no answer says what they hold, a count of none or of many; `never_fetched_by_type` is the same by tab type, a character's `character`: each type that has a location never fetched, its `tabs` every location of the type and `fetched` how many of them were, so `tabs` minus `fetched` were never fetched, and `items` the live items in the fetched ones, counted as the `tabs` tool counts a type, over the scope's realm in every league whatever the query asks, since what a never-fetched location holds is unknown, and `tabs` lists a league's tabs, `never_fetched_leagues` naming the leagues that hold a never-fetched tab, the most first), `terms` (for each term how many items matched, failed, lacked the thing or were undecided — each term alone over every item in scope, never over the matches, which are `total`; a pattern term's `resolved` is what it matched there, its `rest.denominator` `scope`). Rows past the limit are counted in `view.rows.left_out`. Any request an answer holds is sent back as printed.

- `basis` (object) — Carried by a continuation, as an answer's `next` holds it; never written by hand.
- `context` (string) — "matches" (the default), or "corpus": the view over every item in scope, the query set aside.
- `count` (integer) — Beside `request`, the count it was printed with: the answer then says under `before` where the count has moved.
- `counted_at` (object) — Beside `request` and `count`, the `basis` of the answer the count came from: the answer then says under `before` wherever the basis has moved.
- `query` (object) — {"text": a query in the language `describe` prints} or {"tree": the tree an answer returned}, never both; omitted, every item in scope. Or {"trade": a trade site search's URL, id or query JSON}, translated with no network; `describe` with names ["trade"] says what it becomes and reports.
- `request` (object) — In place of the fields above: a request as an answer holds one — a count's, `view.rows.next`, an offer — sent whole, as printed.
- `scope` (object) — What is searched: {"account": needed only where several could be meant, "realm": pc, xbox, sony, poe2 or all, needed only where the store holds several, "membership": "live" (the default), "all", the removed items too, each marked with when it left, or "removed"}.
- `view` (object) — {"rows": {"limit": N, "sort": a value, "desc": true}}, the default (20 rows); {"counts": {"keys": [a key, …], "sum": a value}}; {"cross": {"keys": [two keys]}}; or {"explain": {"path": a node's path}}; "routes": true beside any. `describe` with names ["answer"] says what each gives.

### `set_sync_policy`

Write the account's sync policy, validated strictly, compare-and-swap on `if_revision`. Local only: it sends nothing; the network cost appears only when a plan is applied. Each realm the value names replaces that realm alone, the others stay; `replace: true` writes the whole policy. Returns the stored row, and `league_notes` for a league the account's fetched league list does not hold, with `list_fetched_at` (null when never fetched; a `leagues` job fetches it). Nothing refuses on a note.

- `account` (string | null) — A username, with or without its #discriminator, or a uuid; needed only when several are known.
- `if_revision` (integer | null) — The revision this write replaces, from sync_policy: required when a policy exists, `replace` or not; omitted only to create the first.
- `replace` (boolean | null) — true writes the whole policy: every realm `value` does not name is removed. Default false: the realms `value` names, the rest kept.
- `value` (object, required) — The policy, e.g. {"version":3,"realms":{"pc":{"leagues":{"Standard":{"tabs":"all","characters":"all","max_age_seconds":3600}}}}}: per league `tabs` and/or `characters`, "all" or an id list; a realm named with no leagues is removed. Validated strictly, landing whole or not at all.

### `shop_render`

The forum shop pages for one league, for a person to paste by hand; this server posts nothing and reads no forum (no daemon). One link code per hand-priced item under its price's spoiler, in pages of at most `size` characters: `pages` (each `text` is what to paste, and its `tabs` name each `Stash<n>` its links write — the tab's rank among the tabs the website lists — by the tab's own `name`, with how many `items` link to it), `posted`, `left_out` (the first 50 items left off for a reason, the blocked first, each with its `cell`) and `left_out_total` counting them all, `counts` (every item by its cell; the items nothing applies to in `off_page`), `policy` (each cell's rule and count) and `freshness`: whether the sync policy covers the posted items, reported, never enforced.

- `account` (string | null) — A username, with or without its #discriminator, or a uuid; needed only when several are known.
- `expand` (boolean | null) — List every item left off in `left_out`, past the first 50 and the items nothing applies to included (some 200 bytes an item; a league holds thousands); they are counted either way.
- `league` (string | null) — The league; Standard when omitted.
- `page` (integer | null) — One page alone, by its number from 1: that page's record in place of the whole render.
- `realm` (string | null) — pc (the default), xbox, sony or poe2.
- `size` (integer | null) — Characters per page, the template included: the forum's hard limit when omitted.
- `template` (string | null) — The page's text, holding `[items]` exactly once, which each page replaces with its items; omitted, a page is the items alone.

### `show`

One item as the search derives it, live or removed (no daemon): its place, its fields, class, category and price, and every line with its kind, template and numbers. `against` adds why the item does or does not match a query, each node's outcome (`describe` with names ["against"]); `body` adds the stored body, verbatim.

- `account` (string | null) — A username, with or without its #discriminator, or a uuid; needed only when several are known.
- `against` (object) — Why the item does or does not match a query: `{"text": …}`, `{"tree": …}` or `{"trade": …}`, as a request's `query`.
- `body` (boolean | null) — Also return the body as the store holds it, verbatim.
- `id` (string, required) — An item's id, whole, as an answer printed it.

### `store_status`

Store file path, size, and row counts (no daemon). `leagues` counts the league listing's rows and `league_list` says whether that listing was ever fetched (`acq leagues` or a `leagues` job fetches it); characters and tabs carry their league names whether or not it was. `refused_bodies` counts bodies the store would not take, which `acq store refused` lists. `withheld_responses` counts fetches of tabs or characters the latest list no longer named and `withheld_items` the items they carried: kept as evidence, in no search or count, nothing to do unless a list names the place again. `unlifted_items` counts item-shaped objects in characters' data under a key this build does not read as items, so no search counts them: GGG added a kind of item list, which a newer build reads. `granted_skills` counts the skill entries PoE2 characters' items grant — GGG shows a granted skill once in each place it appears, the item's sockets and the character's skills, so one skill can be two entries — kept with their characters and not counted as items, having no id of their own.

- `account` (string | null) — A username, with or without its #discriminator, or a uuid; needed only when several are known.

### `submit_job`

Submit a job to the daemon and return its id at once; poll job_status, then job_result. `describe` with names ["daemon"] says what the job tools answer.

- `account` (string | null) — A username, with or without its #discriminator, or a uuid; needed only when several are known.
- `kind` (string, required) — Job kind, one a client may submit: profile, characters, character, leagues, stashes, stash, apply, sleep, fetch, whoami. sleep, fetch, whoami run against the mock daemon alone and are refused in real mode. apply_plan sends a reviewed plan, with the checks an apply here skips.
- `params` (object) — Kind-specific params, e.g. {"league": "Standard", "id": "..."} for stash. Passed verbatim; visible to every connected client.
- `priority` (integer | null) — Higher runs sooner (default 0).

### `sync_policy`

The account's sync policy (declared coverage and freshness) with its revision — what refresh_plan compiles (no daemon). `policy` is null when none is set.

- `account` (string | null) — A username, with or without its #discriminator, or a uuid; needed only when several are known.

### `tabs`

The stash tabs of a league, counted first, from the store (no daemon): how many tabs, fetched, never fetched and folders, how many items, and the same by tab type (`by_type`, the most items first); then `rows`, the tabs listed — every one where the league holds thirty or fewer, else the ten with the most items (`listed`: `all` or `most_items`) — each with its type, item count and fetch time. `left_out` counts the tabs not listed. `type` and `never_fetched: true` select the tabs listed — the never fetched of one type by name, which a refresh takes — under the league's counts, `selected` counting them. A name ending ` (Remove-only)` is GGG's mark on a tab whose items can only be taken out.

- `account` (string | null) — A username, with or without its #discriminator, or a uuid; needed only when several are known.
- `expand` (boolean | null) — List every tab of a league of more than thirty, or of the selection, in place of the ten with the most items.
- `league` (string | null) — League name; defaults to "Standard".
- `never_fetched` (boolean | null) — Only tabs never fetched.
- `realm` (string | null) — Realm: pc (default), xbox, or sony.
- `type` (string | null) — Only tabs of this type, as GGG names it (UniqueStash, MapStash, PremiumStash …; `by_type` lists them).
