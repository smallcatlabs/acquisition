## Where the first reader's evidence did not stand
- C148, clause 2: the first reader names the type as holding the clause, and its own doc (held.rs:1-9) says "never under `shared`". The type does neither. `CredentialsHeld` proves the credentials lock is held while an index write or keyring persist runs. It does not prove `shared` is not also held, and keyring or index reads take no token. That is why clause 1 is `open` above.
- C166: the first reader did not confirm that every response goes through `write_line_within`. I checked: every frame write in server.rs goes through `write_line` (server.rs:1217), so the evidence stands and is stronger.
- Every other holder I opened asserts what the report says: seat_faults.rs:3634, documents.rs:1660-1772, :3986 and :4031, harness/mod.rs:282 and :371-395, and arch-check.sh:555.

## Noticed
- C49 and C17 have no clause the first reader marked P, so the brief's step 1 has nothing to ask of them. But C49 clause 1 ("only the session layer interprets accounts") does bind new code: a new `if account == …` anywhere in the daemon outside the session layer fails nothing. Asked as a P clause, it would be `open`.
- C3 clause 7 is met by the CLI alone. No "a daemon was started" words exist in crates/acquisition-mcp. If a mock-mode MCP job tool spawns a daemon and is refused for want of a login, its refusal may not say so. That would be a breach today, not one a new thing makes. Low confidence: I did not drive it.
- The CLI's `connect` and `connect_as` (crates/acquisition-cli/src/run.rs:54, :65) are the doors that make C3 clause 7 and C51 clause 5 hold. They are conventions: plan_cmd and web call `frontend.connect()` and `note_spawn` by hand. Routing every CLI connect through `run::connect`, or making `note_spawn` part of `Frontend::connect`'s result, would turn both rows to `caught`.
- C76: a new page that ticks tabs and submits `stash` jobs one by one through `jobs::submit` would be a second door, and nothing refuses it (`submit` is open to frontends for `acq submit`). I left the row `bound` because the clause names the plan path. If the promise is read as "every selection", the row is `open`.
