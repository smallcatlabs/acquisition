Commands named in brackets. All figures come from read-only runs on the tree at 73e7e77c. Anything marked "(judgment)" is my opinion, not a measurement.

### 1. The five findings that matter most

1. **The one-home map no longer covers what sessions produce.** `audits/` holds 2,442,020 committed bytes across 168 files [`git ls-files -z audits | xargs -0 cat | wc -c`]. `roadmap/reviews/` holds 388 files, 105 of them `-prompt.md` copies of REVIEW-PROMPT.md totalling 462,260 bytes [`ls … | wc -l`; `cat roadmap/reviews/*-prompt.md | wc -c`]. `search/` commits 44,212,552 bytes [`git ls-files -z search | xargs -0 cat | wc -c`]. AGENTS.md "Where things live" names none of these three directories. The only stated rule for `audits/` is the seat skill's `audits/<slice>/seat/` (.claude/skills/seat/SKILL.md:19), yet the 2026-11-09 semantic-model read landed there too, and nothing cites it.
2. **REVIEW-CHECKLIST.md only grows.** 77 shapes have ever existed in its history and all 77 are still there; none has been removed or renamed [python set-diff over `git log -p`]. Its 19 deleted `| **` lines were all in-place edits. For 32 of 77 rows, "Where it came back" names a single occurrence such as `N2`, `platforms` or `plan 7's review` [regex proxy over column 2]. That contradicts the head's own "shapes of fault that came back" (judgment on the proxy).
3. **`runs/` breaks the live-run skill's own check.** live-run SKILL.md:125 says `ls -t runs/ | head` against RUN-LEDGER.md: "a bundle newer than the last row is a run that happened off the record". Today the four newest bundles are mutation and census evidence (`151-component-waits-after`, `151-component-waits`, `transport-seam-after`, `transport-seam-close`), not live runs [`ls -lt runs`]. So the check now gives a false alarm on every read.
4. **The old "authored master-side" ground-truth model survives in three places**, after the rehome audit flagged seven such statements (audits/rehome-2026-10/contradictions/findings.tsv X1–X7):
   - RUN-LEDGER.md:17, in the head that every live-run session reads.
   - tools/tracer-rung.sh:952, which prints "anything learned about GGG goes to ground truth master-side" to every tracer session.
   - search/pseudo-stats/README.md:335.

   AGENTS.md:132 and live-run SKILL.md:168 now say the opposite.
5. **The generated user references are the healthiest home.** 73 of 74 CLI-REFERENCE.md commits since 10-19 also touched `crates/*/src` [`git show --stat` loop]; the one that did not is 1e4d0632, a test-layout move. Both references carry a "Generated by … Do not edit" head, and `readme_tour.rs` runs the README.

### 3. What earns its keep

- **The generated references:** they change with their source in 73 of 74 commits, and their heads name the regenerate command.
- **SURFACES.md:** read by 10 tools (category-table.py, trade-sheet.py and others) and two skills. It is a working register, not prose.
- **search/:** cited by about 50 files in `crates/` and `tools/`; tests read its data. Its "Rules in force" (search/README.md:20) is a self-contained process home that works.
- **SEAT-LEDGER.md:** 11 rows of 756 B median (from the stats over `^|` lines), each pointing into `audits/<slice>/seat/`, which 10, 10, 11 and 4 outside files cite for league, readiness, answer-facts and web respectively [grep]. The evidence trail is real and read.
- **REVIEW-CHECKLIST.md is wired.** REVIEW-PROMPT.md:16 reads it, every `-prompt.md` copy carries it, and docs-check governs it.
- **Ground truth:** 43 of 52 N-claims are cited from code, decisions, skills or ledgers; 36 from crates and tools alone [python grep]. The nine uncited are N15, 21, 22, 31, 32, 37, 41, 45, 50 [same command], and N51 is cited only in roadmap/README.md. No maintained duplicate of either ground-truth file exists in this tree [`git ls-files | grep ground-truth`]. The "acquisition-qt takes copies" line appears twice, word for word (AGENTS.md:132, live-run:168).
- **HISTORY.md:** its "Record whole at" column is unique. For the 12 slices before 2026-10-20 it is the only index to the deleted records (e.g. `944406ff`, `b6197a82`). That is not in roadmap/LEDGER.md or in git log subjects.
- **TESTING-NOTES.md:** eight named identifiers all exist (`assert_journal_holds` in 4 files, `start_with_clock` 2, `MockPolicy` 1, `RefreshOwnerGuard` 2, the c161 test 1, `mockggg.rs`, `goldens/journey/a_first_session.txt`, `unit/tests/`) [grep -rl over crates; ls]. It describes no dead mechanism among those checked.

- **Empty root directories.** `daemon/` and `ratelimit/limiter.rs/` (a directory) were both created 2026-10-29 16:22 [`ls -la`]. Git does not track them, and .gitignore does not ignore them. They mirror crate paths, so probably a tool's stray mkdir (judgment). Nothing names them.
- **audits/search-stage-5-audit-results.md** (10,968 B) is a pre-root-swap Codex review. It links absolute `…/spikes/…` paths that no longer exist, and nothing cites it (0 citers).
- **audits/semantic-model-2026-11** (176,194 B, 2026-11-09): nothing cites it yet, and it is a read, not a seat. That makes it the first non-seat in `audits/` since the relicense and rehome entries, with no stated rule.
- **audits/rehome-2026-10** is 562,472 B with 5 citers. Its largest files are test-census TSVs (107,676 + 68,620 + 46,997 B) [`wc -c` sort], spent working data (judgment).
- **roadmap/reviews:** 105 verbatim REVIEW-PROMPT copies (462 KB). Each differs only in its commit line.

The two-clocks story likewise sits in both the checklist's "A stress that never reaches…" row and TESTING-NOTES "Two clocks in one test".

- **tools/tracer-rung.sh:952** says the same thing, printed at runtime.

- **The live-run skill's off-the-record check** (SKILL.md:125): the newest `runs/` entries are not live runs, as in finding 3.

### 6. Ideas, at most five

1. Add `audits/`, `roadmap/reviews/` and `search/` to "Where things live", with one line each saying what goes in them.
2. Keep one REVIEW-PROMPT.md and record the prompt's commit sha in each review's head, instead of a 462 KB set of copies.
3. Give the checklist an exit: a row whose "Held by" is a structural check retires through C154.
4. Move non-live bundles to a separate gitignored directory, or narrow the skill's `ls -t runs/` check to `runs/20*`.
5. Fix the three "master-side" lines and the five-trigger list in CONTRIBUTING.md together, and delete the empty `daemon/` and `ratelimit/` directories.
