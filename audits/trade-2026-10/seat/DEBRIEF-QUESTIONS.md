# The debrief

Your report is written. Answer these through the door's `debrief`
(`--new` for the first part), each under its number, citing call
numbers where you can and marking an answer "impression" where you
cannot. The same rules bind as for the run: no file but the ones your
brief allows, no command but the door's.

1. Where did the tool stop you, and what did you do instead?
2. Which outputs did you have to read twice, and what made them hard?
3. What did you want to ask a person, and when?
4. Which of the tool's own words misled you, and what did you take
   them to mean?
5. What would you change first, and why that first?
6. What did you never find out?
7. What should we have asked you that we did not, and what would you
   add to any answer above?

Your last message: at most 150 words — that the debrief is written,
and the one answer you would most want read.
